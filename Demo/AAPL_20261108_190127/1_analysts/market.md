## 2. Why these eight indicators

I deliberately paired one indicator per job to avoid redundancy:

| Job | Indicator(s) | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Primary trend | **close_200_sma** | Slow, authoritative regime filter; distinguishes bull market from distribution |
| Intermediate trend / dynamic support | **close_50_sma** | The level that has contained every meaningful pullback since August |
| Short-term momentum & entry timing | **close_10_ema** | Fastest read on whether buyers are still in control day-to-day |
| Trend/momentum crossover | **macd** (+ snapshot signal) | Captures the shift from accelerating to decelerating momentum |
| Overbought/oversold & divergence | **rsi** | Independent oscillator; used here for divergence, not absolute level |
| Overhead volatility envelope | **boll_ub** | Defines the breakout zone and whether price is riding or rejecting the band |
| Risk calibration | **atr** | Converts volatility into stop distance and position size |
| Volume confirmation | **vwma** | Distinguishes price moves backed by volume from drift; identifies underwater buyers |

Skipped as redundant: `boll` middle (duplicates the 20-SMA already implied by 50-SMA context and given in the snapshot), `boll_lb` (taken from the verified snapshot rather than a separate series), `macds`/`macdh` (derivable from MACD plus the snapshot), and no second oscillator (e.g., stoch RSI) to avoid double-counting momentum.
